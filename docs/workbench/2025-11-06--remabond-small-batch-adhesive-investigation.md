# REMABOND Small-Batch Adhesive Investigation

## Context

A small-batch REMABOND alternative to SC-2000 was sought for bonding rubber to carbon in a monofin leading edge.

The discussion was narrowed to REMA TIP TOP products and explored:

- SC-2000;
- SC-4000;
- SC-BL;
- FIXPASTE VC-4 with SC-BL; and
- using SC-BL by itself.

The user corrected the response after it repeatedly described SC-BL as a two-component adhesive: SC-BL is supplied as one part.

## Product distinctions

### SC-2000

- Two-component CR-based adhesive used with UT-R20 hardener.
- Intended for industrial rubber bonding.
- The source material places trichloroethylene-based products in a category offered outside the European Union.
- The response called SC-2000 “banned in the EU,” which was stronger than the source established.

### SC-4000

- Two-component CR-based adhesive used with ER-42 hardener.
- Trichloroethylene-free.
- Intended for rubber-to-rubber, rubber-to-fabric and rubber-to-metal applications.
- The official catalogue lists a 350 g black package, making it smaller than some SC-2000 packages but still an industrial product.

Product reference: <https://www.rema-tiptop.com.au/product/cement-sc-series/>

### SC-BL

- A separately supplied patch cement available in approximately 200–225 g packages.
- Used as part of the FIXPASTE VC-4 repair system.
- The reviewed manufacturer documentation does not identify a separate hardener for SC-BL.
- No manufacturer information found in the discussion established SC-BL as a standalone structural rubber-to-carbon adhesive.

Product reference: <https://shop.rema-tiptop.com.au/cement-special-bl-220g-cfc-fr-ee>

### FIXPASTE VC-4 with SC-BL

FIXPASTE VC-4 is the actual two-component repair compound:

- `VC4-A` and `VC4-B` are mixed as the repair material.
- SC-BL is supplied alongside them as the associated cement.
- The system is intended to fill damage in rubber components based on NR, SBR, IR, BR or their blends.
- Small sets include 150 g each of components A and B plus 40 g of SC-BL.
- Larger sets include 650 g each plus 225 g of SC-BL.

Brochure reference: <https://www.rema-tiptop.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/REMABOND-Adhesive-Systems-REMA-TIP-TOP-Australia.pdf>

## Advice received

The response proposed SC-4000 as the closest modern REMABOND alternative to SC-2000.

It also proposed:

- SC-BL plus FIXPASTE VC-4 for filling, rebuilding or sculpting rubber;
- SC-BL alone for thin rubber-to-carbon bonding; and
- roughening and cleaning both surfaces before applying one or two thin coats of SC-BL.

The response claimed that standalone SC-BL would create a strong, flexible and waterproof rubber-to-carbon bond after 24–72 hours.

## Editorial notes

- No product was selected, purchased or tested.
- SC-BL was initially and incorrectly described as two-component.
- The later explanation that it contains a latent, moisture-reactive hardener was generated without documentary support.
- The claims that SC-BL alone chemically bonds carbon, self-vulcanizes independently, matches SC-4000 strength and resists saltwater or UV were not established by the cited REMA documentation.
- FIXPASTE VC-4’s documented rubber compatibility does not include EPDM or carbon composites.
- The suggested SC-BL-alone procedure and timings are not retained as validated instructions.
- The supplied PDF supports using SC-BL with FIXPASTE VC-4, not the proposed monofin application.
- Non-REMABOND alternatives are omitted because the comparison was explicitly narrowed.

The durable conclusion is that **SC-4000 is the documented general-purpose REMABOND adhesive**, while **SC-BL is a small-package cement associated with FIXPASTE VC-4 whose suitability as a standalone rubber-to-carbon adhesive remained unresolved**.
