# Small-Batch Alternatives to SC-2000

## Context

A smaller-package alternative to SC-2000 was sought for bonding rubber to carbon or fibreglass in small DIY fin projects.

The conversation produced a list of potential products but recorded no purchase, compatibility test or adopted alternative.

## Candidate products

### Bostik 2402 with hardener

The strongest documented candidate from the list:

- Two-component, polychloroprene-based contact adhesive.
- Intended for rubber-to-metal and other rubber assemblies.
- The manufacturer describes resilient bonds with resistance to water, humidity, heat and UV exposure.
- A 250 ml plus hardener package is documented for marine applications.
- Compatibility with the actual rail rubber and carbon laminate would still require testing.

Product reference: <https://www.bostik.com/uk/en_GB/markets-applications/transportation-aerospace/marine/leisure-marine/inflatable/>

### 3M Scotch-Weld 847

- Single-component nitrile-rubber adhesive.
- Intended for flexible bonds involving nitrile rubber, plastics and gasket materials.
- The response grouped it with neoprene adhesives, but 3M identifies its base as nitrile rubber.
- No evidence was provided for EPDM-to-carbon bonding or repeated saltwater immersion.

Technical-data reference: <https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/1630219O/847-ds-eng-bnl-06-2017.pdf>

### 3M Scotch-Weld 1300/1300L

- Single-component polychloroprene contact adhesive.
- Intended to bond several rubbers to other substrates.
- Available in different viscosities.
- The manufacturer’s documentation does not establish it as a direct substitute for SC-2000 or validate this fin application.

Technical-data reference: <https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/66251O/neoprene-hi-perf-rubber-gasket-adh-1300-1300l.pdf>

### TECH vulcanizing cement

- Intended for tyre-repair and rubber-vulcanizing applications.
- Manufacturer information supports rubber repair uses, not bonding rubber rails to carbon.
- It remains a research lead rather than a recommended substitute.

Product reference: <https://products.techtirerepairs.com/product/temvulc-quart/>

### Other generated leads

The response also named:

- REMA TIP TOP Blue Cement;
- Pang P-200 rubber cement; and
- Loctite 480 rubber-toughened cyanoacrylate.

The first two were described as SC-2000-like alternatives without supporting technical information. Loctite 480 was proposed for quick repairs or small bonding areas rather than as a flexible, large-area replacement.

## Editorial notes

- None of these products was selected or tested.
- “Nearly identical to SC-2000” and “same chemistry as SC-2000” were unsupported comparisons.
- Tyre-repair cement designed for rubber-to-rubber use should not be assumed to bond carbon composites.
- The actual rubber compound remained unidentified.
- Package size and regional availability may determine whether an option is genuinely suitable for a two-fin batch.
- This note is a shortlist for coupon testing, not a construction procedure.
